Project Discription

We picked the BL21 transformed single clone containing plasmid 5, inoculated it in LB containing antibiotics to grow overnight, diluted to OD600 of 0.1 with fresh LB, and cultured in shake flask. We incubated it at 37°C to an OD600 of 0.8, added 0.5 mM isopropyl-d-mercaptogalactoside (IPTG) for induction, and it grew at 250rpm-300rpm for 3 h at 30°C to overexpress the protein. Cells were harvested by centrifugation and resuspended in M9 liquid medium pH 5 with necessary antibiotics, 1% glucose, 20 mM naringenin, 0.5 mM IPTG, 0.1 mM 2-oxoglutaric acid, 2.5 mM sodium ascorbated at 30°. We continued incubation at C for 48h (you can choose the feed at the 24th hour in the middle: 1% glucose (20ul), 2uM naringenin), and then centrifuged it to observe the color of the colony (add HCL to show pink, add NaOH to show grass green).

The coding genes of the first two proteins are driven by the first T7 promoter of the vector for transcription, and the coding genes of the latter two proteins are driven by the second T7 promoter of the vector for transcription, and finally translated into four corresponding proteins.

Among these proteins, F3H catalyzes the conversion of naringenin to dihydrokaempferol, F3'5'H catalyzes the conversion of dihydrokaempferol to dihydromyricetin, DFR catalyzes the conversion of dihydromyricetin to leuco delphinidin, while ANS Catalytic converts of colorless delphinidin to colored delphinidin.
