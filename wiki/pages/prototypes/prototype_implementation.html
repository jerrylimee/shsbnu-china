Our school and even most of the middle school students in the world have myopia or eye damage, and there are also a lot of eye care products on the market. We are thinking about how to combine our projects with eye care.

The anthocyanins produced by our project are blue or purple, and can be made into anti-blue light products.

So we are like anthocyanins-used for contact lenses.

We use anthocyanins to prevent harmful blue lights-anthocyanins are blue and purple can prevent electronic products of harmful blue light, blue and purple contact lenses or cosmetic contact lenses can also be used as decoration.

Anthocyanin is a pure natural substance that is harmless to the human body, and even has benefits such as relieving blurred vision, eye distension, fear of light, and dry eyes.
