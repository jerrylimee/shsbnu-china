Who are we?


We are a group of students from Second High School attached to Beijing Normal University, International Division. Joined by many elites who devoted themselves to the study of bioscience, we work as a family with the same goal and determination. We help each other and work as a family. We are SHSBNU_China.

Our team's vision is to collaborate with teams from all over the world in iGEM - this expansive international platform. Our project - synthesizing delphinidin - is based on the experiences we learnt in this collaboration process. Our product is planned to be applied to dyeing techniques, vision therapy and many other fields.

Our team's goal is to synthesize different enzymes using synthetic biology techniques that convert coumaric acid to naringenin and then delphinidin in seven processes.

The HP group members are investigating the public perception of synthetic biology and making contacts with the factories related to our product. At the same time, the modeling group is assist in the experimental group to analysis the experimental results, while the art group is now representing the progress of our experiment on different online platforms and designing logos to do propaganda of our team. Education related activities are also in progress.
